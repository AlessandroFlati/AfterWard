2. CHECK FOUR INDEPENDENT FIELDS FOR EACH medicine / cream / rehabilitation TASK
For each field decide whether it is documented, genuinely absent, or not applicable. Check all four separately, using the original pages:
- amount: the prescribed quantity per dose/application/session. Product strength, package count and tool capacity do not establish the prescribed quantity. 'Use the measure' lacks quantity; 'one measure per application' supplies quantity.
- frequency: how often a dose/application/session recurs. 'Per dose', 'per application' or 'per session' does NOT specify recurrence. Course length, an inpatient demonstration time and blank diary logging slots do not establish frequency. A dated range does not establish frequency either.
- start: the task explicitly says when to begin or continue. A first date, continuation instruction, return-home trigger, authorization call or equipment-fitting trigger counts. A trigger can be in the future without a known date: do not demand a calendar date in addition. 'At home' only specifies setting. A discharge date or a statement that the patient returned home does not establish the task's start without an explicit link. 'For N days from the first dose' gives duration but alone does not say when to take the first dose.
- duration: FIRST determine whether the task is explicitly chronic/ongoing or a limited course. Chronic/ongoing treatment does not need a fixed end: never report missing_duration just because there is no stopping date. For a limited course, a length, dated end or independently identifiable stop/review event suffices. 'Stop at the end of the cycle/course' is circular if that cycle's length/end is not defined elsewhere. 'Temporary' and package size do not define it.
A dated treatment range establishes start AND duration. A home instruction may explicitly continue an earlier schedule; otherwise ward-only entries do not supply home fields. Report each absent applicable field, even if another gap for that task has already been found. Do not apply these four checks to visits, forms, contacts or equipment logistics.

3. RESOLVE EACH REQUIRED DOCUMENT REFERENCE
For every specific sheet/form/card required by an active task, search all pages for that document's actual usable content. Match its identity and purpose, not just a similar code, a shared topic or the word 'form'.

- Content absent: only its title, description of its purpose, index entry, handover receipt, collection signature or statement of possession appears. A different form, stock sheet or receipt does not satisfy the reference. Report missing_attachment under the owning task even if a recipient is supplied, and even if another necessary field is also missing.
This means absent from supplied evidence, not proof it was never handed to the patient. The human workflow can ask for the paper copy. Do not invent a document requirement from oral teaching, package labels, a diary to be written in future, or a printed section number.
