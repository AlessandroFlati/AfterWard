TREATMENT FIELDS: medicine, cream and rehabilitation only
- amount = quantity per administration/application/session. A tablet's strength, box contents, or measuring tool capacity is not a prescribed quantity. 'Use the supplied measure' does not state how many measures. 'One measure per application' does.
- frequency = how often administrations/applications/sessions recur. 'Per application' and 'per session' define a unit, not recurrence. A total course length does not establish sessions per day. Blank diary rows and logging slots do not prescribe a schedule.
- start = an explicit instruction linking the task to its first date, continuation or trigger. 'At home' is a setting. A nearby discharge date, return-home statement or prior demonstration does not establish start unless the task is explicitly linked to it. 'Start on returning home' does. 'Only after authorization' or 'after equipment fitting' is a complete event-based start, even when that event has not happened and has no date yet. 'For N days from the first application' defines length, not what triggers that first application.

An explicitly chronic/ongoing treatment is also a documented duration policy: cite those words as duration, without inventing a stopping date. A temporary course ending 'when the course is complete' is circular if its length/end is nowhere defined: do not tag that phrase as duration. Packaging does not establish course length.
