Six pages for the PDF pitch

  1. The problem. A discharge letter says to use an attached sheet. The patient cannot find it and does not know when to start. The ward receives fragmented questions without the full conversation. Visual: one letter, two practical questions.
  2. The product. AfterWard keeps documents, questions, replies and follow-ups in one discharge case. The ward can begin before discharge; the patient can bring a document when the hospital has not adopted the service. Visual: the three entry routes.
  3. One concrete story. Show the letter, a question about the rehabilitation sheet, the ward sharing it and replying, then a follow-up in the same thread. The patient confirms understanding. Use screenshots from the guided case.
  4. Technology with visible evidence. Google Gemma-4-31B-IT transcribes scanned pages through the AMD API. Show original and transcription side by side with actual request time. The server enforces quotas and validates response structure; a person checks the text. Show the live document check, its exact source citations and the selected question becoming a conversation. Document checking uses medium reasoning, with two passes and a conditional conflict comparison. Explanations require staff review; reply drafts require explicit staff sending. Historical research metrics are not a benchmark of this English release. Do not claim direct Instinct, ROCm, Quark or AITER execution without the corresponding verified evidence.
  5. Business value and cost discipline. The intended buyer is a ward or hospital. The business hypothesis is less time reconstructing fragmented conversations and answering avoidable repeat calls. Model choice, prompt size and number of calls affect consumption; provider pricing affects the cost of that consumption. Keep those two mechanisms separate. Use measured token usage and a dated, sourced paid-price assumption for estimates; free trial credits do not establish a durable unit price. No measured staffing or readmission savings are claimed.
  6. What judges can try and what comes next. Link the public repository and live demo. List the three guided cases and current limitations. Next gates: validate the integrated analysis with real users under suitable data-processing terms, and integrate verified hospital identity and delegation.

Repeatable video sequence

Target a 3-4 minute edit. Record the longer OCR and document-check waits in full first, then shorten it visibly if needed. Do not present an edited delay as a latency measurement.

  1. Opening, about 20 seconds. “Leaving hospital should not mean losing the conversation. AfterWard helps patients and wards keep discharge documents and unresolved questions together.”
  2. The patient, about 40 seconds. Open the PDF, run its document check and select the source-linked question about the rehabilitation sheet. “The letter can be correct and still leave a practical question unanswered.”
  3. The ward, about 60 seconds. Open the same case, share the existing sheet, review an AI reply suggestion and send the selected draft. “The ward already has the documents. It can respond directly; uploading extra evidence is optional.”
  4. The follow-up, about 35 seconds. Ask for more detail, answer in the same thread and resolve it as the patient. “The conversation stays connected until the patient understands.”
  5. The AMD and Google path, about 45 seconds. Upload the supplied scan. Show Gemma transcription, source comparison and review. “Google Gemma runs through AMD. Here you can inspect both the model output and the document it came from.”
  6. Close, about 20 seconds. “Try the guided cases yourself. This release connects source-reviewed OCR, document checks and human-reviewed replies. Clinical validation and hospital deployment remain explicit next gates.”

Recording checklist

  • Use a new demonstration session and the English example PDFs.
  • Keep a second tab open for the ward. Use an invite if recording two browsers or devices.
  • Verify AMD OCR is enabled and a real request succeeds before recording.
  • Capture the initial letter, connected thread, batch-send confirmation and original/transcription comparison.
  • Keep personal documents, account dashboards, credentials and private development pages out of the recording.
  • Export the final pitch PDF with working repository and demo links; check every page at presentation size.
